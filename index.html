Automated Recon Pipeline

Argus 5-Phase Native Recon Engine

The custom pipeline engineered to map entire attack surfaces methodically before strategic exploitation.

1

Phase 1: Passive OSINT

Asset & subdomain harvesting via Subfinder, Findomain, Amass, and Assetfinder.

2

Phase 2: Active Brute-Force

DNS & endpoint brute-forcing with custom wordlists utilizing Gobuster and FFuf.

3

Phase 3: Permutations

Dynamic permutation synthesis using DNSGen and mass resolver queries with MassDNS.

4

Phase 4: Probing & Validation

Live HTTP/HTTPS service validation, title extraction, and deduplication via HTTPX & anew.

5

Phase 5: Deep Tech & Vulns

Fingerprinting technologies, WAF detection, and vulnerability scanning via WhatWeb, Wafw00f, Nmap, and Nuclei.
